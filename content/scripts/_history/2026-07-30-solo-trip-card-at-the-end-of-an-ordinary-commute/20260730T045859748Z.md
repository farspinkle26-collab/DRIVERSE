## Hook Options

- Nothing happened on this drive. That is the whole point. **(chosen)**
- Twenty-two minutes I won't remember. Something else does.
- This is the most boring drive I'll do all week.

## Script

[0:00–0:02] Hook, spoken flat over the road ahead. No cut.
"Nothing happened on this drive. That's the whole point."

[0:02–0:07] Ordinary commute — indicator, a slow lane change, the same roundabout as every other day.
"Same road, same time. Twenty-two minutes I won't remember."

[0:07–0:12] Pull up. Engine off. Phone comes into frame already open.
"Except something's keeping score now."

[0:12–0:16] APP MOMENT — the trip card, distance and duration settling in. Thumb moves past it; it does not linger.
"[FILL: read the real distance off the card — never a made-up number] for going to work."

[0:16–0:20] Door open, phone into pocket, walk out of frame.
"Tomorrow's the same drive. It just counts now."

## Visual Direction

Camera: dash-mounted phone, centre-right, lens just above the wheel rim so the road fills two thirds of frame. The phone running Driveverse stays out of shot until 0:07.
Mount: suction mount low on the windscreen — readable, never across the driver's sightline.
App moment: 0:12–0:16. Four seconds, one continuous take, mid-action. The trip card is already on screen when the phone enters frame — no unlocking, no navigating, no menus, no demo.
Light: late afternoon, sun behind the car. Screen at ~70% or the card blows out on camera.
Cuts: three, all under one continuous audio bed — the drive should feel unedited.

## Filming Checklist

- [ ] Mount phone dash-visible — screen readable, not blown out
- [ ] Clean the windscreen and the phone lens
- [ ] Drive the route once before filming so the driving looks ordinary
- [ ] Start the drive in-app so a real trip is being recorded
- [ ] End the drive before filming the card — the card is the payoff, not a menu
- [ ] Film the app moment in 3–5 seconds, mid-action — never a demo
- [ ] Don't narrate the app: no "and here you can see…"
- [ ] Shoot one clean take of the hook on its own as a spare
