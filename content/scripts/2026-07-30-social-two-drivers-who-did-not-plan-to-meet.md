## Visual Direction

Camera: dash mount, centre, tilted to keep the mirror in frame — the other car has to arrive in shot, not be cut to.
Mount: same low windscreen position as the Solo scripts; keep the app screen legible at night by dropping brightness to ~50%.
App moment: 0:03–0:08, three to five seconds, one take, mid-drive. The map is already open — no unlocking, no zooming, no pointing at it.
Night: no interior light. Screen glow is the only light on the driver.
Cuts: two. The meet itself must be one unbroken shot or it reads as staged.

## Filming Checklist

- [ ] Mount phone dash-visible — screen readable, not blown out
- [ ] Clean the windscreen and the phone lens
- [ ] Confirm real drivers are on the map right now — a staged meet-up reads as staged
- [ ] Agree the meet point with the other driver off-camera, not in the shot
- [ ] Open Driveverse before starting the recording, not on camera
- [ ] Confirm the map shows nearby activity, not an empty map
- [ ] Film the app moment in 3–5 seconds, mid-action — never a demo
- [ ] Don't narrate the app: no "and here you can see…"
- [ ] Shoot one clean take of the hook on its own as a spare
