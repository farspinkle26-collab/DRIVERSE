## Visual Direction

Camera: dash mount, centre-right, road two thirds of frame. The notification has to arrive inside the shot — do not cut to it.
Mount: low windscreen, screen angled slightly toward the lens so the notification is legible without leaning.
App moment: 0:09–0:13, four seconds. A quest must already be in progress before recording starts; waiting for the fire on camera is dead air.
Light: overcast or shade — a notification banner blows out in direct sun.
Cuts: two, both before the notification. After it lands, nothing is cut.
