# Company Registration System Documentation

## Overview
This document outlines the complete company registration system for Towing Online, designed to ensure only verified and professional towing companies can join our platform.

## Registration Process Flow

### 1. Initial Application
- Company submits registration through the mobile app
- System validates basic information
- Draft is saved automatically for later completion

### 2. Document Verification
- Legal documents are reviewed by our verification team
- Background checks are performed
- Insurance and licensing verification

### 3. Quality Assessment
- Fleet inspection (virtual or physical)
- Driver qualification review
- Service capability evaluation

### 4. Contract Signing
- MoU/Contract is digitally signed
- Commission structure is agreed upon
- Service level agreements are established

### 5. Platform Integration
- Company profile is activated
- Drivers are linked to company account
- Payment and settlement systems are configured

## Required Data Fields

### Company Information
- **Company Name**: Legal business name
- **Business Registration Number**: NIB/SIUP number
- **Tax ID (NPWP)**: Indonesian tax identification
- **Email**: Primary business email
- **Phone**: Business contact number
- **Address**: Complete business address including province
- **Website**: Company website (optional)
- **Established Year**: Year company was founded
- **Employee Count**: Number of employees

### Contact Person
- **Full Name**: Primary contact person
- **Position**: Role in company (Owner, Director, Manager)
- **Phone**: Direct contact number
- **Email**: Personal email address
- **ID Number**: KTP number for verification

### Legal Documents
- **Business License**: SIUP/NIB document
- **Company Registration**: Akta Pendirian
- **Tax Certificate**: NPWP document
- **Insurance Certificate**: Company insurance (optional)
- **Operating Permit**: Transportation permit (optional)
- **Contact Person ID**: KTP of contact person

### Fleet Information
- **Total Vehicles**: Number of tow trucks
- **Vehicle Details**: Type, brand, model, year, license plate
- **Vehicle Photos**: Multiple angles of each vehicle
- **Registration Documents**: STNK for each vehicle
- **Insurance Documents**: Vehicle insurance certificates
- **Maintenance Schedule**: How often vehicles are serviced

### Driver Information
- **Total Drivers**: Number of drivers employed
- **Employment Types**: Full-time, part-time, freelance breakdown
- **Training Programs**: Driver training and certification
- **Driver Requirements**: Company standards for drivers

### Services Offered
- Light Vehicle Towing
- Heavy Vehicle Towing
- Motorcycle Towing
- Emergency Roadside Assistance
- Battery Jump Start
- Tire Replacement
- Accident Recovery
- Insurance Partner Service

### Pricing Structure
- **Pricing Model**: Per km, per trip, hourly, or custom
- **Base Rate**: Starting price
- **Per KM Rate**: Additional charges per kilometer
- **Minimum Fee**: Minimum charge per service
- **Emergency Multiplier**: Extra charge for emergency calls
- **Night Time Multiplier**: Extra charge for night services
- **Holiday Multiplier**: Extra charge for holidays
- **Insurance Discount**: Discount for insurance partners

### Payment Methods
- Cash acceptance
- Card payment capability
- E-wallet integration (GoPay, OVO, DANA)
- Bank transfer options

### Operations
- **Service Areas**: Cities/regions covered
- **Operating Hours**: 24/7 or specific hours
- **Emergency Hotline**: 24/7 contact number
- **Response Times**: Urban, suburban, rural response times
- **GPS Tracking**: Vehicle tracking capability
- **Fleet Management**: Software used for fleet management

### Banking Information
- **Bank Name**: Primary business bank
- **Account Holder**: Account name
- **Account Number**: Business account number
- **Branch**: Bank branch (optional)
- **SWIFT Code**: For international transactions (optional)

### Technology Integration
- **Driver App**: Mobile app for drivers
- **Customer App**: Customer-facing application
- **Towing Online Integration**: Willingness to integrate
- **API Capability**: Technical integration capability
- **GPS Provider**: GPS tracking service provider

### Business References
- **Insurance Partners**: Insurance companies worked with
- **Corporate Clients**: Major corporate clients
- **Government Contracts**: Government work experience
- **Experience**: Years in towing business

### Agreements
- Terms and Conditions acceptance
- Data processing consent
- Quality standards agreement
- Commission structure acceptance
- MoU signing

## Verification Process

### Document Verification
1. **Legal Document Check**
   - Verify business license authenticity
   - Check tax registration status
   - Validate company registration documents

2. **Identity Verification**
   - Verify contact person identity
   - Cross-check with government databases
   - Confirm authorization to represent company

3. **Financial Verification**
   - Bank account verification
   - Credit check (if applicable)
   - Financial stability assessment

### Operational Verification
1. **Fleet Inspection**
   - Vehicle condition assessment
   - Safety equipment check
   - Insurance coverage verification

2. **Driver Qualification**
   - License verification
   - Background checks
   - Training certification review

3. **Service Capability**
   - Coverage area verification
   - Response time validation
   - Equipment adequacy assessment

## Quality Standards

### Vehicle Requirements
- Vehicles must be in good working condition
- Safety equipment must be present and functional
- Insurance coverage must be current and adequate
- Regular maintenance records must be maintained

### Driver Requirements
- Valid driving license (SIM A/B)
- Clean driving record
- Professional appearance and behavior
- Customer service training

### Service Standards
- Response time commitments must be met
- Professional service delivery
- Transparent pricing
- Customer satisfaction maintenance

### Safety Standards
- Proper safety equipment usage
- Traffic law compliance
- Accident prevention protocols
- Emergency response procedures

## Commission Structure

### Standard Commission Rates
- **Light Vehicle Towing**: 15% platform fee
- **Heavy Vehicle Towing**: 12% platform fee
- **Emergency Services**: 18% platform fee
- **Insurance Partner Jobs**: 10% platform fee

### Payment Terms
- Weekly settlements for new companies
- Monthly settlements after 6 months of good standing
- Instant payment for premium partners

### Performance Bonuses
- High rating bonus: 2% reduction in commission
- Volume bonus: Tiered discounts based on monthly jobs
- Exclusive area rights: Reduced commission for exclusive coverage

## Termination and Suspension

### Grounds for Suspension
- Repeated customer complaints
- Safety violations
- Document expiration
- Non-compliance with standards

### Termination Process
- Written notice with specific reasons
- Opportunity to address issues
- Final settlement of outstanding payments
- Account deactivation

This comprehensive system ensures that only qualified, professional towing companies join the Towing Online platform, maintaining high service standards and customer satisfaction.
