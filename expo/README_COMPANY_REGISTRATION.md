# 🚛 Towing Online - Company Registration System\n\nA comprehensive, professional company registration system for towing companies joining the Towing Online platform. This system ensures only verified and qualified companies can onboard, maintaining high service standards and customer satisfaction.\n\n## 📋 Overview\n\nThe Company Registration System is a multi-step verification process that collects comprehensive information about towing companies, validates their credentials, and establishes partnership agreements. The system is designed to be scalable, secure, and user-friendly while maintaining strict quality standards.\n\n## ✨ Key Features\n\n### 🔐 **Comprehensive Verification**\n- Legal document validation\n- Business license verification\n- Insurance coverage confirmation\n- Fleet inspection and documentation\n- Driver qualification assessment\n\n### 📱 **Multi-Step Registration Process**\n- **Step 1**: Company Information\n- **Step 2**: Contact Person Details\n- **Step 3**: Legal Documents Upload\n- **Step 4**: Fleet Information\n- **Step 5**: Driver Information\n- **Step 6**: Services Offered\n- **Step 7**: Pricing & Payment Methods\n- **Step 8**: Operations Details\n- **Step 9**: Banking Information\n- **Step 10**: Technology Integration\n- **Step 11**: Business References\n- **Step 12**: Agreements & MoU Signing\n\n### 🎯 **Quality Assurance**\n- Service level agreements\n- Performance monitoring\n- Customer satisfaction tracking\n- Regular compliance audits\n\n### 💼 **Professional Integration**\n- Digital MoU/Contract signing\n- Commission structure management\n- Payment settlement automation\n- Platform integration support\n\n## 🏗️ System Architecture\n\n### **Frontend Components**\n- `EnhancedCompanyRegistrationScreen`: Main registration interface\n- `useCompanyRegistrationStore`: State management hook\n- Enhanced form components with validation\n- Progress tracking and step navigation\n\n### **Backend Structure**\n- Supabase database with comprehensive schema\n- Row-level security policies\n- Document storage and management\n- Real-time status updates\n\n### **Data Models**\n```typescript\ninterface CompanyRegistration {\n  companyInfo: CompanyInfo;\n  contactPerson: ContactPerson;\n  documents: LegalDocuments;\n  fleet: FleetInformation;\n  drivers: DriverInformation;\n  services: ServiceOfferings[];\n  pricing: PricingStructure;\n  operations: OperationalDetails;\n  bankInfo: BankingInformation;\n  technology: TechnologyIntegration;\n  references: BusinessReferences;\n  agreements: LegalAgreements;\n}\n```\n\n## 📊 Required Data Collection\n\n### **Company Information**\n- Legal business name and registration\n- Tax identification (NPWP)\n- Complete business address\n- Contact details and website\n- Company establishment details\n- Employee count and structure\n\n### **Legal Documentation**\n- Business License (SIUP/NIB)\n- Company Registration (Akta Pendirian)\n- Tax Certificate (NPWP Document)\n- Insurance Certificates\n- Operating Permits\n- Contact Person ID (KTP)\n\n### **Fleet & Operations**\n- Vehicle inventory with specifications\n- Vehicle registration documents (STNK)\n- Insurance coverage for each vehicle\n- Maintenance schedules and procedures\n- Service area coverage\n- Operating hours and emergency contacts\n\n### **Financial & Legal**\n- Banking information for settlements\n- Pricing structure and commission agreements\n- Insurance partnerships\n- Corporate client references\n- Legal compliance documentation\n\n## 🔄 Registration Process Flow\n\n```mermaid\ngraph TD\n    A[Company Starts Registration] --> B[Fill Company Information]\n    B --> C[Add Contact Person Details]\n    C --> D[Upload Legal Documents]\n    D --> E[Register Fleet Vehicles]\n    E --> F[Add Driver Information]\n    F --> G[Select Services Offered]\n    G --> H[Set Pricing Structure]\n    H --> I[Configure Operations]\n    I --> J[Add Banking Details]\n    J --> K[Technology Integration]\n    K --> L[Business References]\n    L --> M[Sign Agreements]\n    M --> N[Submit for Review]\n    N --> O{Document Verification}\n    O -->|Approved| P[Account Activated]\n    O -->|Rejected| Q[Revision Required]\n    O -->|Needs Info| R[Additional Documents]\n    Q --> B\n    R --> D\n    P --> S[Platform Integration]\n    S --> T[Driver Onboarding]\n    T --> U[Service Launch]\n```\n\n## 🛡️ Quality Standards\n\n### **Vehicle Requirements**\n- Vehicles must be in good working condition\n- Current compliance\n- Insurance and liability coverage\n- Regular audit and review processes\n\n## 🔧 Technical Implementation\n\n### **Database Schema**\n```sql\n-- Company Registrations Table\nCREATE TABLE company_registrations (\n  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),\n  owner_id UUID REFERENCES auth.users(id) NOT NULL,\n  status TEXT CHECK (status IN ('draft', 'submitted', 'under_review', 'approved', 'rejected', 'requires_revision')) DEFAULT 'draft',\n  company_info JSONB NOT NULL,\n  contact_person JSONB NOT NULL,\n  documents JSONB NOT NULL,\n  fleet JSONB NOT NULL,\n  drivers JSONB NOT NULL,\n  services JSONB NOT NULL,\n  pricing JSONB NOT NULL,\n  payment_methods JSONB NOT NULL,\n  operations JSONB NOT NULL,\n  bank_info JSONB NOT NULL,\n  technology JSONB,\n  references JSONB,\n  agreements JSONB NOT NULL,\n  submitted_at BIGINT,\n  reviewed_at BIGINT,\n  approved_at BIGINT,\n  reviewed_by UUID REFERENCES auth.users(id),\n  rejection_reason TEXT,\n  revision_notes TEXT,\n  created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW(),\n  updated_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW()\n);\n```\n\n### **Security Policies**\n- Row-level security enabled\n- User can only access their own registrations\n- Admin users can review all registrations\n- Audit trail for all changes\n- Document access controls\n\n### **API Integration**\n- RESTful API endpoints\n- Real-time status updates\n- Document upload handling\n- Notification system integration\n- Third-party verification services\n\n## 🚀 Getting Started\n\n### **For Companies**\n1.
