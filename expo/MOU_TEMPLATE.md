- [X] Light Vehicle Towing (cars, motorcycles, small trucks)

### 3.3 Driver Requirements
- Employ minimum [TOTAL_DRIVERS] qualified drivers
- Ensure all drivers have valid licenses (SIM A/B)
- Provide driver training and certification
- Maintain professional appearance and behavior standards

### 3.4 Operational Requirements
- Operate [OPERATING_HOURS] as specified
- Maintain emergency hotline: [EMERGENCY_HOTLINE]
- Achieve response times: Urban [URBAN_RESPONSE]min, Suburban [SUBURBAN_RESPONSE]min, Rural [RURAL_RESPONSE]min

## ARTICLE 4: FINANCIAL TERMS

### 4.1 Commission Structure
Towing Online shall retain the following commission from each completed service:
- Light Vehicle Towing: 15% of service fee
- Heavy Vehicle Towing: 12% of service fee
- Emergency Services: 18% of service fee
- Insurance Partner Jobs: 10% of service fee

### 4.2 Pricing Structure
- Base Rate: IDR [BASE_RATE]
- Per Kilometer Rate: IDR [PER_KM_RATE]
- Minimum Fee: IDR [MINIMUM_FEE]
- Emergency Multiplier: [EMERGENCY_MULTIPLIER]x
- Night Time Multiplier: [NIGHT_MULTIPLIER]x
- Holiday Multiplier: [HOLIDAY_MULTIPLIER]x

### 4.3 Payment Terms
- Settlement Period: [Weekly/Monthly]
- Payment Method: Bank transfer to [BANK_NAME] Account [ACCOUNT_NUMBER]
- Payment Timeline: Within 7 business days of settlement period
- Currency: Indonesian Rupiah (IDR)

### 4.4 Performance Bonuses
- High Rating Bonus (4.5+ stars): 2% commission reduction
- Volume Bonus: Tiered discounts based on monthly completed jobs
  - 50+ jobs: 1% commission reduction
  - 100+ jobs: 2% commission reduction
  - 200+ jobs: 3% commission reduction

## ARTICLE 9: TERM AND TERMINATION

### 9.1 Agreement Term
- Initial term: 2 years from signing date
- Automatic renewal: 1-year periods unless terminated
- Early termination: 90 days written notice

### 9.2 Termination Grounds
Either party may terminate for:
- Material breach of agreement terms
- Insolvency or bankruptcy
- Loss of required licenses or permits
- Failure to meet quality standards

### 9.3 Termination Process
- Written notice with specific reasons
- 30-day cure period for correctable breaches
- Final settlement within 30 days
- Return of confidential information

## ARTICLE 10: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### 10.1 Negotiation
Disputes shall first be resolved through good faith negotiation between authorized representatives.

### 10.2 Mediation
If negotiation fails, disputes shall be submitted to mediation through the Indonesian Mediation Center.

### 10.3 Arbitration
Unresolved disputes shall be settled through binding arbitration under Indonesian Arbitration Rules.

### 10.4 Governing Law
This agreement shall be governed by Indonesian law and regulations.

## ARTICLE 11: GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 11.1 Amendment
This agreement may only be amended through written consent of both parties.

### 11.2 Force Majeure

### 11.3 Severability

This MoU constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements.
